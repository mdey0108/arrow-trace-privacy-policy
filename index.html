1. Introduction

Welcome to Arrow Trace: Line Puzzle ("App", "we", "us", or "our"), published by Twenty5Apps. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it. 2. Information We Do NOT Collect

Arrow Trace is built around privacy-first, serverless principles. The App does not require user registration, logins, or social account linkage. Specifically:

  • We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, or physical address.
  • We do not collect or track your precise GPS location.
  • We do not access your contacts, camera, microphone, or external media files.
  • We do not operate a centralized server to store player profiles or behavioral dossiers.

3. Local Device Storage

All game data and preferences are saved locally and exclusively on your personal device using standard private storage systems (such as Android SharedPreferences and iOS NSUserDefaults). This data includes:

  • Campaign progress, unlocked levels, and star ratings across difficulties.
  • In-game currency (Coins) balance, lifetime stats, and achievement milestones.
  • Unlocked cosmetic trail skins and visual themes.
  • Locally generated custom levels created using the Level Studio.
  • Audio volume and haptic vibration preferences.

You can clear this data at any time via the in-game Settings menu ("Reset All Progress") or by clearing the App's storage via your device settings.

4. Third-Party Services & Advertising (Google AdMob)

Arrow Trace is a free-to-play game supported by non-intrusive mobile advertising provided by Google AdMob (a service provided by Google LLC). To deliver advertisements appropriate for your region and prevent fraudulent ad traffic, Google AdMob SDK may automatically collect certain device and technical information, including:

  • Device Identifiers: Google Advertising ID (GAID on Android) or Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA on iOS).
  • Device & Network Data: Device model, operating system version, network connection type, and general IP-based approximate location (country/city level).
  • Ad Interaction Metrics: Information regarding ad impressions, clicks, and rewarded video completion.

All ad network communications are secured using industry-standard TLS/HTTPS encryption. For full details on how Google processes ad data, please visit:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services

5. Personalized Ads & Opt-Out Options

You have full control over interest-based advertising on your mobile device:

  • Android: Go to Settings > Google > Ads, then select "Delete advertising ID" or "Reset advertising ID".
  • iOS: Go to Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking, and disable tracking permissions.

6. Device Permissions Used

Arrow Trace requests only minimal, necessary system permissions:

  • INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Required solely to fetch and render ads from Google AdMob servers. The game itself remains 100% playable offline when disconnected.
  • VIBRATE: Used to provide tactile haptic vibration feedback for puzzle steps, rotations, and completed levels (can be toggled off in Settings).
